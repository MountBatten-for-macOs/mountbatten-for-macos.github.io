MountBatten

A disk and volume firewall for macOS. MountBatten continuously monitors mounting events, letting you precisely control which physical drives and disk images can access your Mac.

Precise Control

Define rules based on volume names or UUIDs. Block unauthorized drives silently or trigger custom responses on match.

Flexible Actions

Respond with alerts, notifications, sounds, screen flashes, app launches, URLs, AppleScripts, or shell scripts with parameters. Log everything without disruption.

Secure & Reliable

Touch ID protection prevents tampering. Automatic log rotation at 10MB keeps your records clean and performant.

Real-Time Awareness

Event Log auto-scrolls to the latest entry. Stay informed on every mount attempt as it happens.

Effortless Setup

Install and configure in moments. Runs quietly in the background, safeguarding your system without intervention.

How It Works

Step 1: Define Rules

Create rules matching volume names or UUIDs for drives and disk images you want to control.

Step 2: Set Actions

Choose responses like blocking, alerting, running scripts, or logging the event.

Step 3: Monitor Live

MountBatten watches in real-time. View auto-scrolling logs and enable Touch ID for security.

Step 4: Stay Protected

Your Mac remains secure from unauthorized mounts, with rotated logs for ongoing oversight.

FAQ

What does MountBatten monitor?

All disk mounting events, including physical drives and disk images, using names or UUIDs.

Can I customize responses?

Yes: alerts, notifications, sounds, flashes, app launches, URLs, AppleScripts, or shell scripts.

Is it secure?

Touch ID locks settings. Logs rotate automatically at 10MB for reliability.

Does it slow my Mac?

No. MountBatten runs efficiently in the background with minimal resource use.
